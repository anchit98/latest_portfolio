## 9. Product philosophy (for an About or "How I work" section)

1. **Problem before technology.** The sequence should be Problem → Evidence → Experiment → Product, not AI → Feature → Product.
2. **Data over hierarchy.** Seniority should not replace evidence. Anchit frames disagreement around user data, business impact, adoption, existing behavior, effort, expected value, and experiments. Preferred line: "Here is what the data suggests." over "I don't think we should do this."
3. **Automation is not automatically successful.** Hours saved or FTE reduction is not enough proof. Hierarchy: Savings → Adoption → Retention → Quality/CSAT → Business Outcome. If users don't adopt it, a theoretical efficiency number means little.
4. **AI does not eliminate product judgment.** AI speeds up research, documentation, prototyping, analysis, coding, feedback synthesis, and workflow design. Humans still decide what problem is worth solving, for whom, why now, what not to build, which trade-offs are acceptable, and where autonomy should stop.
5. **Use the least autonomous system that reliably solves the problem.** Predictable workflow: traditional automation. Needs language understanding: LLM workflow. Genuinely dynamic, multi-step: may justify an agent. More autonomy is not automatically a better product.

Core belief: As execution becomes cheaper, product judgment becomes more valuable.
